## DETAILED DAILY SCHEDULE  

### **Day 1 – Arrival & First Taste of Tokyo**  
**Morning** – Arrive at **Haneda (HND)** (non‑stop 11‑12 h from LAX/SFO/SEA). Clear immigration, collect JR Pass (if you’ll use it) and a pocket‑Wi‑Fi device.  

**Transport to hotel** – Take the **Keikyu Line** to **Shinjuku** (≈ 30 min, ¥590).  

**Check‑in** – **Hotel Gracery Shinjuku** (mid‑tier, central, 9‑min walk to Golden Gai).  

**Afternoon**  
* 13:00 – Lunch at **Tsukiji Outer Market** (fresh sushi, grilled seafood).  
* 15:00 – Stroll through **Shinjuku Gyoen** (shaded garden, great for a hot‑day break).  

**Evening**  
* 18:30 – Walk through **Omoide‑Yokocho** (tiny izakayas).  
* 20:00 – Explore **Kabukichō** neon streets; optional **Robot Restaurant** show (indoor, air‑conditioned).  

**Tip:** Buy a **Suica/Pasmo** IC card for sub‑way rides you’ll use later.

---

| Time | Activity |
|------|----------|
| 08:00 | Breakfast at hotel (Japanese set) |
| 09:00 | **Tsukiji Outer Market** – sample tamago‑yaki, grilled scallops |
| 11:00 | **Ginza** – high‑end shopping, visit **Mitsukoshi** food hall (cool indoor) |
| 13:00 | Lunch at **Ginza Kyubey** (sashimi) |

| 18:30 | Return to central Tokyo (Yurikamome line) |
| 20:00 | **Asakusa** – Senso‑ji temple (evening illumination), stroll Nakamise‑dōri (souvenirs) |
| 21:30 | Dinner at **Daikokuya Tempura** (famous tempura) |

**Evening cool‑down:** Grab a **matcha ice‑cream** at **Asakusa Kagetsudo**.

---

### **Day 3 – Hakone & Mt Fuji Views**  

**Morning**  
* 07:00 – Early breakfast, check‑out.  
* 07:45 – Take the **Romancecar** from Shinjuku Station to **Hakone‑Yumoto** (≈ 85 min, ¥2,280, seat‑reserved).  

**Mid‑day** – Purchase a **Hakone Free Pass** (covers most local transport).  

**Itinerary**  
* 09:30 – **Hakone Open‑Air Museum** – sculptures, indoor galleries (cool).  
* 11:30 – **Owakudani** – volcanic valley, try black‑egg (cooked in sulfur).  
* 13:00 – Lunch at **Hakone Gora Brewery** (local craft beer + ramen).  

**Afternoon** – **Lake Ashi** cruise (30 min) → **Hakone‑Machi** → **Hakone‑Tozan Railway** to **Gora**.  

**Evening** – Check‑in at **Gora Kadan** (luxury ryokan, includes private onsen and kaiseki dinner).  

**Night** – Relax in the onsen, enjoy a traditional **kaiseki** multi‑course dinner (included).  

**Why a ryokan?** You’ll get an authentic Japanese night‑time experience and a chance to cool off in hot‑spring water—perfect after a hot day.

---

### **Day 4 – Shinkansen to Kyoto & Fushimi Inari**  

**Morning**  
* 07:30 – Light breakfast (ryokan).  
* 08:30 – Board the **Shinkansen Hikari** from **Odawara** to **Kyoto** (≈ 2 h 15 m, ¥13,080 with JR Pass).  

**Arrival in Kyoto** – Drop luggage at **Hotel Gracery Kyoto Sanjo** (central, near Kawaramachi).  

**Mid‑day**  
* 11:30 – **Fushimi Inari Taisha** – hike the iconic torii‑gate mountain (start early to avoid heat).  

**Lunch** – Try **Yudofu** (tofu hot‑pot) at a small restaurant near the shrine.  

**Afternoon**  
* 14:30 – Walk through **Higashiyama** district (covered streets, tea houses).  
* 16:00 – **Kiyomizu‑dera** (sunset view over the city).  

**Evening**  
* 18:30 – Stroll **Gion** – spot geisha, enjoy a **kaiseki** dinner at **Gion Karyo** (≈ $70 pp).  

**Night** – Optional **Pontocho** alley for a cocktail bar (air‑conditioned).

---

### **Day 6 – Nara Day‑Trip → Osaka Nightlife**  

**Morning – Nara**  
* 07:30 – Breakfast, check‑out, store luggage at Kyoto station lockers.  
* 08:00 – JR Nara Line to **Nara** (≈ 45 min).  

**Nara Highlights**  
* **Todai‑ji Temple** – Great Buddha (indoor, air‑conditioned).  
* **Nara Park** – friendly deer (early morning, less heat).  
* **Kasuga Taisha** – lantern‑lined path (shaded).  

**Lunch** – Try **kakinoha‑zushi** (sushi wrapped in persimmon leaves) at a local shop.  

**Afternoon – Transfer to Osaka**  
* 13:30 – Take the **Kintetsu Nara Line** to **Osaka‑Namba** (≈ 40 min).  

**Osaka Highlights**  
* **Osaka Castle** (park, some shade).  
* **Umeda Sky Building** (observatory, air‑conditioned).  

**Evening – Dōtonbori**  
* 18:30 – Check‑in at **Swissôtel Nankai Osaka** (directly above Nankai Namba Station, great for night transport).  
* 19:30 – Dōtonbori food crawl: **takoyaki**, **okonomiyaki**, **kushikatsu**.  
* 21:30 – River‑front walk, neon lights, optional **Karaoke** in a private room (cool).  

**Night** – Return to hotel; enjoy the rooftop bar for a nightcap.

**Morning – Osaka**  
* 07:30 – Breakfast at hotel.  
* 08:30 – Quick visit to **Shinsekai** (Tsutenkaku Tower) for a panoramic view.
